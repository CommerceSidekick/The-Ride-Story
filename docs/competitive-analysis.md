# The Ride Story: competitive analysis (November 2026)

Sources are linked inline. Prices are US/EU list prices and vary by region. Anything we could not confirm is marked unverified.

## What changed recently

- **Strava API (June–Sept 2026).**
  - Standard-tier developers need a Strava subscription.
  - New apps start at 1 athlete, and can go up to 10 without review.
  - Club endpoints were removed.
  - Data can be cached for at most 7 days.
  - Data may not be used in AI applications.
  - An athlete's data may be shown only to that athlete. ([Terra](https://tryterra.co/blog/strava-api-changes-2026), [Strava API Agreement](https://www.strava.com/legal/api))
- **Strava paywalled its recaps.** Year in Sport became subscriber-only in Dec 2025 ([road.cc](https://road.cc/content/news/strava-year-sport-now-only-subscribers-317425)). Flyover is subscriber-only ([Strava](https://support.strava.com/hc/en-us/articles/19900004650125-Flyover)).
- **FATMAP shut down** on Oct 1, 2024 ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2024/06/26/strava-to-shutter-3d-mapping-platform-fatmap-18-months-after-acquisition)).
- **Komoot** was bought by Bending Spoons in 2025. There were large layoffs, and Premium is now €59.99/yr ([BikeRadar](https://www.bikeradar.com/news/komoot-redesign-2025)).
- **Relive** removed Communities, Routes and Goals ([Relive](https://support.relive.com/kb/guide/en/your-new-relive-is-here-SkECJOTybj/Steps/4393615)).
- **Polarsteps** launched Plus on July 14, 2026. It includes 3D Play mode and up to 10 travel buddies, and is pitched at bikepackers ([Polarsteps](https://www.polarsteps.com/news/polarsteps-launches-polarsteps-plus-a-new-subscription-for-its-most-dedicated-travelers)).
- **Ride with GPS** added 3D maps on paid tiers in Sept 2026 ([the5krunner](https://the5krunner.com/2026/09/17/ride-with-gps-3d-maps/)).
- **Newcomers:** How It Went ([Product Hunt](https://www.producthunt.com/products/how-it-went)), PicTrek, TripReel and TrailsPals.

## Why we win, and for whom

1. **The crew is the input.**
   - No one else merges several riders' recordings of the same ride and pools everyone's photos on one timeline.
   - For MTB trip crews, gravel and bikepacking groups, moto touring groups and tour operators.
2. **One upload, three outputs.** An online story, ready-to-post sets and a ride-specific book with maps, elevation and stats. Photo-book makers have no route data; travel-journal books have no ride data.
3. **The 3D story is free where rivals charge.** Strava Flyover, Relive 3D, Polarsteps Play and Ride with GPS 3D are all behind subscriptions.
4. **Any device.** GPX, FIT and TCX from Strava, Garmin, Wahoo, Coros, a phone or a moto GPS. Polarsteps can't import GPX, and moto apps keep rides inside their own app.
5. **Built for multi-day trips.** A chapter for every day. Strava and Relive work one activity at a time.
6. **Moto is underserved.** Every moto app is navigation-first. None tells the story or prints it.

## Threats

- **Polarsteps is the closest.** It has buddies, 3D, print and a bikepacking push. GPX import would close most of the gap.
- **Relive** has the 3D and photo engine, but has been cutting social features, not adding them.
- **Strava** controls the data and can tighten the API again or bundle group recaps into its subscription.
- **Indie apps** (How It Went, PicTrek) ship fast, but they are solo and video-only.

**Defensible edges:**
- Merging duplicate recordings, and matching photos to the route when every camera's clock is slightly off.
- A loop built into the product: every invite brings in new riders, and every shared story carries the sponsor and a sign-up link.
- Print layouts made for rides.
- Working with any device.
- The print margin.

**Strava risk.** Keep file upload as the main path and Strava as a per-rider convenience. Ask Strava in writing about group display. Never feed Strava data to AI.

## Brand distance from Strava

Strava's brand is orange (#FC5200) on white and black. Its API Agreement asks connected apps to keep a design distinct from Strava's look and feel.

Our original accent (#FF5A1F) sits very close to Strava orange. The site now supports five palettes, set in `src/lib/palettes.ts`; three of them avoid orange entirely (Summit gold, Glacier ice blue, Signal lime). Preview any of them with `?palette=<id>`.
